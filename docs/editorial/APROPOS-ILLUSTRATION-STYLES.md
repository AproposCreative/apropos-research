# Apropos illustration styles

## Status and reference basis

Editorial preferences supplied 10 September 2026. This is a reusable art-direction
brief, not model fine-tuning, a deployed image-generator configuration or a claim
of a perfect style match. The attached `aproposcolorstyle.jpg` was visually read
in the conversation. Dropbox directories were not opened or imported.
Minimal Drawing is based on the user's description; an actual minimal-style

## Shared rules

- One idea, one focal subject and at most a few supporting elements.
- A coherent scene or standalone drawing, never a collage, montage, cutout
  scrapbook, split-panel layout or collection of article keywords.
- Empty space is part of the composition. No decorative props just to fill it.
- Editorial wit comes from pose, scale or a meaningful contrast.
- No text, logos, watermarks, stock-photo styling, polished 3D or glossy rendering.
- Landscape editorial delivery target: 1920 × 1080. Check actual exported
  dimensions and crop; never stretch a portrait reference into landscape.
- References guide the visual vocabulary, not compulsory subject matter.
  The pasta, woman, sunglasses and clothing in the example are not a template
  to repeat in every illustration.
- Generated interpretation of an exhibition must not masquerade as a documentary
  photograph or establish new facts about the artwork.

## 1. Minimal Drawing

Simple, classic, cool. A single readable subject on a quiet light background,
restrained hand-drawn linework, economical detail and generous negative space.
Suggested starting palette: ink plus paper, optionally one small color accent.
The palette and exact line character remain provisional until a minimal-style
reference is supplied. Do not make minimal mean generic icons or clip art.

## 2. Expressive

Colorful yet visually disciplined. The supplied reference has flat cobalt-blue,
pink and yellow areas, black outlines and black masses, red accents, and a pale
cream background. Colors can be saturated; the surface remains matte and grainy.
This supersedes any blanket requirement that all Apropos illustrations use muted
colors. Palette names are visual estimates, not measured brand color values.

Use confident, slightly irregular contours and economical internal detail.
Visible fine grain unifies the flat fills and paper background; avoid glossy
gradients or elaborate photorealistic shading. One main action anchors the image.
Texture must not muddy the silhouette or replace intentional drawing.

## THIRST TRAP starting concept

Proposed, not generated or approved: one oversized gold/ochre rat beside one small
park statue, on a largely empty pale background with a restrained patch of green.
The scale contrast carries the feature's idea about what a city permits to take
up space. Expressive is a suitable first study; do not pack the rat, patterned
ticks, carnivorous plants, skyline, phones and festival references into one image.
Use the supplied exhibition photographs for subject cues only. Any reinterpretation
is an editorial illustration, not an exact reconstruction of the installation.

## Visual acceptance checklist

1. Does the image communicate its single idea at thumbnail size?
2. Is there one clear focal point, with no collage or unnecessary elements?
3. Does the selected style remain distinct: minimal linework or expressive flat color?
4. Are texture, edges and palette deliberate rather than glossy or generic?
5. Are there no accidental letters, malformed anatomy or unexplained objects?
6. Does the final file meet the intended dimensions without distorted proportions?

Review against the user's examples and revise the brief from specific feedback.
Do not mark this checklist passed before an actual image has been generated and inspected.
