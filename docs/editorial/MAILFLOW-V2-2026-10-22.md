not another APPEND. External edits/deletions block MCP send and sync. No two-way
merge, external-send detection or prevention of a human concurrently sending
from Apple Mail; use one sending client at a time. Sent draft cleanup is reported
separately; a cleanup failure cannot resend SMTP.
