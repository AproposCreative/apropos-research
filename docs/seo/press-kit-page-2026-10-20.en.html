Apropos Magazine explores the culture we listen to, watch, read and play. The independent Danish digital culture publication covers music, film, television, books, art, gaming and cultural life through reviews, essays and commentary. Extended description · 150 words

Apropos Magazine explores the culture that fills stages, screens, pages and conversations. The independent Danish publication brings together reviews, essays and commentary on music, film, television, books, art, gaming and cultural life.

Its music coverage includes concerts, festivals and releases. Film and television are considered as storytelling, while books, art and games form part of a wider conversation about aesthetics, identity and society. There is room for both an individual work and the context around it. The editorial voice is personal, curious and open to humour, aiming to make culture matter and welcome fresh perspectives.

Apropos is free to read and carries no advertisements, native advertising or sponsored editorial content. Being free of advertising is a tangible part of its identity, both in the reading experience and in its editorial content.

Published by Apropos Creative ApS, Apropos Magazine is for readers seeking judgments and perspectives across many forms of culture.

Read about Apropos and the editorial team

The works. The experiences. The conversations around them.

Apropos covers culture through reviews, essays, guides and commentary. Its focus is on the works, their expression and the questions they open up: from the form and energy of a concert to a novel's view of society or the way a game tells a story.

Music

Concert reviews, festival coverage, album reviews and guides to acts on the programme. With room for both the music and the culture around the stages.

Film and television

Reviews of films and TV series alongside perspectives on their stories, characters and themes. From an individual release to its place in the wider culture.

Art and design

Exhibitions, artists and visual expression. Coverage includes portraiture, installations and design, where a work's form meets questions about the body, identity and public life.

Books

Book reviews and literary perspectives that consider language, storytelling and the human and social questions raised by literature.

Gaming

Game reviews and perspectives on games as culture. Storytelling, aesthetics and gameplay inform assessments of the worlds that games invite players to explore.

Culture and society

Essays and commentary on subjects including identity, representation, club culture and digital platforms. Cultural phenomena become part of a wider conversation.
