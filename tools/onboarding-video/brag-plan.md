# Apropos AI — intro after connection

Audience: verified Apropos colleagues connecting ChatGPT, often on a phone.
Outcome: know three useful prompts and understand the preview/approval boundary.
Promise: the same editorial tools, inside the conversation. Not autonomous or free API publishing.
Source of truth: MCP v7 tools, chat widget, submissions and welcome-content.ts; no private drafts.
Hook: “Din redaktion. Nu i chatten.” Existing white Apropos logo on black.
Proof: recreated product controls, with fictional article copy explicitly marked “Eksempel”.
Tone: editorial, quiet, direct. No launch metrics, invented testimonials or confetti.
CTA: choose Apropos AI and ask to see your drafts.
Delivery: 36s, 1080-square MP4, burned-in Danish text, captions and transcript, linked from one welcome email. Longer than Brag's ad default because this is onboarding and must be readable on mobile.

## Storyboard
0–6: logo, hook, connection to the editorial workflow.
6–12: prompt → two draft rows and one precise missing-image status.
12–18: targeted intro edit → before/after, images retained.
18–24: canonical prompt → illustration in chat; official stills for film/TV.
24–30: full preview → text / images / SEO → price and exact-version approval.
30–36: large starter prompt and CTA.

## Guardrails
No live client data, credentials, private screenshot or claimed successful publishing. No paid generation. No music or voiceover: intentionally silent, suitable for email/mobile, all meaning in text. Brag's external music licensing is not assumed. No third-party telemetry submission, public source upload or social posting. Render locally, host within the existing Apropos deployment.
