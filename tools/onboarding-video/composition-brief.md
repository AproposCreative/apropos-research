# Composition brief

Black/off-white, thin grey rules, existing Apropos artwork. Large generic sans-serif UI typography: 76px headings, 34–46px body, 28px footnotes on 1080px canvas. 64px safe area; no text hidden beneath playback controls. One focal point per beat. Recreated controls use the actual labels “Ret tekst” and “Vælg billeder”; do not pretend to be a recorded publication.

Motion: small 18px entrances over 450ms, a measured transition every six seconds, long settled holds. No camera loops, fake typing, strobing, gradients or ornamental movement. A persistent editorial footer gives progress. Silent by design. All timing deterministic in one paused GSAP timeline, visibility owned by timed scene containers.

First frame and linked email poster must already communicate the product; no blank opening. Review one settled frame from every scene and the local player; run Hyperframes check before render. Capture stays isolated from the user's browser profile.
